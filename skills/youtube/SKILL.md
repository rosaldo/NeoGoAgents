# YouTube

Channel work in the creator's own voice: script, packaging, research, audit. Not for publishing — the human approves every upload.

## Before writing

- Read the creator's voice profile in their project docs if one exists (phrases they use, words they refuse, claims they will not make). If absent, ask for three of their own videos and infer it. A script in the wrong voice is unusable.
- Never invent a number, result or source. Ask for it or write the line without it.
- End every run with a copyable block and one question: ship it, or change it?

## Script

1. Write FIVE hooks against a formula set (contrarian claim, concrete number, question, before/after, mistake). Score them; show the top two with scores, never one.
2. First 15 seconds does three jobs: confirm the click the title promised, open a question, prove the payoff exists.
3. The turn (0:15–0:45): one sentence of what the video will do, then start doing it. No channel intro, no subscribe pitch.
4. Body: one idea per beat, with `[ON SCREEN: ...]` on every beat. A talking head with nothing to look at is a podcast.
5. Payoff stated explicitly, then ONE ask. Estimate runtime at 150 words per minute.

## Packaging

Title and thumbnail are ONE unit — writing them separately makes both say the same thing twice.

- Write ten titles, keep two. Report desktop truncation at 60 characters and mobile at 40 separately; they fail differently.
- Thumbnail: ≤3 words, different words from the title but the same promise, legible at feed size, one number/name/date beats any adjective. Two all-caps words is the ceiling.

## Research and audit

- Rank "what is working" by views divided by that channel's own median, never by raw views. Require ≥4 videos per channel or skip the channel. Public listings only; never a logged-in session or the creator's credentials.
- Audit in this order: last ten titles as a set, thumbnails at feed size, first 15 seconds of the three newest, upload consistency (not frequency), retention shape if exported.
- End an audit with ONE fix, three specific things already working, and what not to do yet. No twenty-item list, no praise you do not mean.

## Scope guard

No upload, scheduling, account login or credential storage. The human publishes.
